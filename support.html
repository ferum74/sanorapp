How to Use

Getting Started with Sanor

Follow these steps to set up and make the most of your medication companion.

1

Download the App

Find Sanor on the App Store, tap Get, and wait for the installation to complete. The app requires iOS 15 or later.

2

Create Your Profile

Open the app and complete a quick setup: enter your name, age, and any relevant health notes. Your data is stored privately on your device.

3

Add Your Medications

Tap "+ Add Medication", enter the name, dosage, and frequency. You can add as many medications as needed.

4

Set Up Reminders

Configure notification times for each medication. Sanor will send you a push notification when it's time to take your next dose.

5

Track Your Daily Intake

Each day, open the app and check off medications as you take them. The calendar view shows your consistency over time.

6

Review Your History & Insights

Visit the History tab to see your adherence trends, streaks, and health notes — useful context to share with your doctor.

7

Connect Apple Health (Optional)

Go to Settings → Apple Health and grant permission to sync your step count, heart rate, and activity data alongside your medication log.
