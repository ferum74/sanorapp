Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026  ·  Effective: May 21, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Sanor ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Sanor mobile application. By using Sanor, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

👤 1. Information We Collect

Sanor is designed with a privacy-first approach. The app primarily stores data locally on your device and does not require account registration.

Information you provide directly:

  • Medication names, dosages, and schedules you enter into the app
  • Health notes and personal reminders
  • Optional profile information (name, age)

Information collected automatically:

  • App usage analytics (anonymized, non-personal)
  • Crash reports and performance data to improve the app
  • Device type and operating system version

🔒 2. How We Store Your Data

All medication and health data you enter is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage. We do not have access to your personal health records.

We use industry-standard encryption to protect data stored on your device. You are in full control of your data at all times.

Future versions of the app may offer optional cloud backup — this will always be opt-in and clearly disclosed.

🚫 3. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties
  • We do not share your health information with advertisers
  • We do not use your data to build advertising profiles
  • We do not share your medication history with insurance companies or employers

🍎 4. Apple Health Integration

With your explicit permission, Sanor may access Apple Health data including:

  • Step count
  • Heart rate
  • Activity and workout data

This data is used only within the app to provide health insights alongside your medication schedule. It is never transmitted to our servers or shared with third parties.

You can revoke Apple Health permissions at any time via Settings → Privacy & Security → Health on your iPhone.

🔔 5. Push Notifications

Sanor uses push notifications to remind you to take your medications. Notification permission is requested within the app and can be disabled at any time in your device Settings.

We do not use push notifications for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

📊 6. Analytics & Crash Reporting

We collect anonymized, aggregated usage data to understand how the app is used and to improve performance. This data does not include your health information, medication details, or any personally identifiable information.

Crash reports may contain technical device information (device model, iOS version, app version) but are never linked to your personal health data.

👶 7. Children's Privacy

Sanor is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

🌍 8. International Users

Sanor is available in multiple countries. If you are located in the European Union, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data.

To exercise your GDPR rights, please contact us using the details below.

⚖️ 9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion — request deletion of your data from our systems
  • Portability — export your data in a readable format
  • Opt-out — disable analytics collection at any time in app Settings

Since most data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all app data by uninstalling Sanor from your iPhone.

🔄 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will notify you through the app or via the App Store update notes.

Continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
