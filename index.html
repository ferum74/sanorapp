Take a tour

Everything you need, calmly in one place.

From the first reminder to the doctor's visit, Sanor keeps your routine simple.

Reminders you can answer in one tap

Mark a dose as Taken, Snooze it or Skip it right from the notification. Forgot? Sanor nudges you again, and you choose how often.

  • Taken, Snooze and Skip in the notification
  • Repeat reminders until you mark the dose
  • Hide medicine names on the lock screen
  • Turn reminders off for a single medicine

Know when to buy the next pack

Enter how many tablets are in the box. Sanor shows how many days they will last, how many you need to finish a course, and warns you before they run out.

  • Days left and the date your supply lasts until
  • A heads-up before the pack is empty
  • One tap on “I bought a pack” to update it

See how doses, symptoms and mood fit together

Doses, symptoms and mood side by side, day by day, for a week, a month or three months, so patterns become easy to notice.

  • Week, month and 3-month views
  • Log symptoms with intensity and notes
  • Optional cycle tracking with predictions

Bring a report to your next appointment

Export a PDF for the last week, last month, all time or any custom range: missed doses, symptoms, mood and averages from Apple Health.

  • Share the PDF with your doctor in a tap
  • Add your daily doses to iPhone Calendar
  • Streaks and adherence at a glance

New in 1.7

Cycle tracking, built around you.

A calm, private way to follow your cycle right next to your medication. Available to everyone: just turn it on in Settings.

  • A ring with your cycle day and what comes next
  • A calendar with period, fertile window and ovulation forecasts
  • A quick log for flow, symptoms, mood and pain
  • Insights on how your cycle varies and which symptoms repeat
  • Forecasts that learn from what you log

Forecasts are estimates based on what you log. They are not a method of contraception and not a medical diagnosis.

New in 1.7

Smarter interaction checks

Sanor now spots two medicines of the same kind (for example two statins or two painkillers from one group), risky combinations by mechanism, and medicines that should be taken at different times. If a medicine could not be checked, the app says so instead of staying silent.

Food and alcohol notes

Short notes on grapefruit, dairy, vitamin K and alcohol right in each medicine’s card.

A medicine database that speaks your language

Find your medicine by the name on the box: thousands of local brand names from Czech, Slovak, Ukrainian, Russian and Spanish registers.

Apple Health, if you want it

With your permission, Sanor shows steps, heart rate and activity next to your medication log. You stay in control in iOS settings.

Fix a mistaken tap

Marked the wrong dose? Undo it right away, or flip a dose between Taken and Missed with one tap.
