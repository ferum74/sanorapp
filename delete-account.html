🗑️ Delete your Sanor account

You can delete your account and all data we hold about you at any time, on iPhone and on Android.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Sanor and go to More → Settings → Delete Account.
  2. Confirm (you may be asked to enter your password, or to sign in again).

This permanently deletes your profile from our database and your sign-in, and clears the app data on your device.

Without the app

If you can no longer open the app, email sanor.help@gmail.com from the address you used to sign up and write "Delete my Sanor account". We will delete your account and profile within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted, and what is not

  • Deleted: your account (sign-in), your name, gender, height, weight, avatar and the records of your consents and settings stored online.
  • Your medications, doses, symptoms, mood and cycle entries are stored only on your phone and are removed when you delete the account in the app or uninstall Sanor.
  • Copies in Google's systems are removed within the periods stated in Google's documentation (generally within about 180 days).

See also the Privacy Policy.
